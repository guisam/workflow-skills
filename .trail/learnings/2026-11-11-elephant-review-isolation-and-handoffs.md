## Decided

The elephant-workbench trial separates continuing design, fresh specification
review and project-specific implementation. Its package records the EGM reference
revision and consulted sources without depending on that plugin. Comprehension
reviews retain a paraphrase; source-backed correctness review is a separate task.

## Tried and ruled out

Do not equate fresh parent-turn context with exclusion of host/project instructions.
Do not transfer the workbench's AP profile to a worker in a consuming project.
Do not treat an exhausted review budget as acceptance of residual objections.
The installed graft CLI lacks `note`; this learning was recorded directly.

## Watch out

The packages were subsequently signed and locally adopted in Agent-Profiles' explicit
elephant role; see docs/elephant-adoption.md. Structural validation, scratch-store
import and fresh MCP delivery do not establish live model behavior. New worktrees may lack ignored adapter/host
configuration and uncommitted design files. Top-level threads and delegated child
tasks have different host contracts. Model and startup choices remain unresolved.

## Subsequent goldfish adoption

Agent-Profiles' external adapter now adds goldfish-comprehension, goldfish-design
and goldfish-readiness, each with its own signed 0.1.0 skill and shared technical
language. Both Codex gpt-6.1-sol and Claude claude-opus-5-5 resolve all three roles;
fresh MCP delivery was checked for all six combinations. No model-specific routing
or default-role changes are needed for these model-neutral roles. T3 selects the
model; each fresh reviewer resolves its assigned AP role. The elephant adapter
role lists the roster, while its immutable signed skill remains unchanged.
See docs/goldfish-adoption.md for verification limits and recovery. The user
reported a live Astra elephant startup; no live goldfish evaluation was run.
